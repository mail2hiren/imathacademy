Enquiries are now in the app

Every teacher gets their own. You can see all of them without having to answer all of them.

1

Your own link

Open the app and go to Enquiries. At the top you will find your link and a QR code.

📱 Enquiries → Copy link

Share the link when someone messages you. Print the QR on a flyer — a parent points their camera at it.

Anyone who uses your link becomes your enquiry. Every teacher has their own.

2

What the parent sees

A short form — the child's name and age, whether they have done abacus before, and how to reach them. About half a minute.

When they press send, WhatsApp opens on their phone with a message already written to you. They press send, and you have a conversation started.

Their details are saved either way — even if they close WhatsApp without sending.
3

Replying

Open Enquiries. There are three tabs:

  • Mine — yours to work on
  • To assign — nobody has them yet
  • Everyone — the whole academy (only you see this)

Tap WhatsApp them and choose what to say:

  • First reply
  • Offer a trial
  • Gentle nudge
  • Fees and timings
  • Welcome them

WhatsApp opens with the message written out. Change anything you like, then send. It goes from your own WhatsApp as normal.

When they join, tap They joined.

4

Giving one to a teacher

On any enquiry there is a dropdown — Assign to… Choose a teacher and it moves to their list. They can then message the parent themselves.

You can also move one that is already with a teacher, which helps when someone is away, or take it back with Take it off them.

Only you can do this. Teachers can see what is waiting, but they cannot take it themselves.
5

Seeing how everyone is doing

The Everyone tab shows each teacher at a glance:

Megha5 · 3 replied · 1 joined
Priya6 · 6 replied · 2 joined
Anjali3 · 0 replied · 3 waiting

“Waiting” is the one to watch. It means a parent has had no reply for two days or more. Those enquiries turn orange in the list so they are easy to spot.

6

On your phone

Your dashboard shows an orange bar whenever someone is waiting:

📬 2 enquiries waiting for a reply
Sunita asked about Aarav 3 days ago

Tap it to go straight there.

One thing worth knowing
The app cannot send WhatsApp messages on its own — WhatsApp does not allow that. It writes the message and opens the chat; you press send. So nothing ever goes out in your name that you have not seen.
