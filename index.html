Megha Bhatt has spent over 20 years mastering and teaching Abacus and Vedic Maths — not as a franchise of someone else's programme, but building her own from the ground up.

She designed the entire iMathAcademy curriculum for both Abacus and Vedic Maths — 10 structured levels, each concept carefully sequenced from her two decades in the classroom. Her curriculum is not adapted from another institution. It is entirely original, built from her own teaching experience with hundreds of children across age groups.

Beyond teaching children, Megha runs a teacher certification programme — training and certifying Abacus teachers who go on to run their own classes. Her programme is trusted by teachers as much as by parents.

Her students span India, UAE, UK and USA. Parents from Dubai and London choose her over local institutes because of the depth and consistency of her teaching. Children who struggle with maths in school routinely go on to win Abacus competitions under her guidance.
